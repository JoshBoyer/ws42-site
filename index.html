The Workshop

One bench. One printer. No rules.

Workshop 42 is a real place — a desk, a Bambu Lab A1 Mini, and whatever problem I'm currently trying to solve with plastic.

Everything here is designed to be practical, printable, and worth your filament. If I wouldn't use it myself, it doesn't make the cut.

This site is the build log. What worked, what failed, and what's still on the bench. Grab a file, follow a build, or just see what happens next.
