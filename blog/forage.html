Building Forage

Updated July 2026 Beta

Type Home management platform for households
Stack React / TypeScript · Fastify · PostgreSQL
Hosting Self-hosted, data kept in Canada
Live site goforage.ca

What It Is

Forage helps a household stay ahead of its home. It learns your home's systems and upkeep, keeps everyone in sync, and plans the week — from the maintenance that's coming due to what's for dinner. It's built for Canadian homes and hosted in Canada. It started as a kitchen tool and grew into the whole house.

Why I Built It

Every home runs on scattered knowledge: when the furnace filter was last changed, what's actually in the freezer, whose turn it is to do what. Forage started with the kitchen — the 4:30 dinner panic, the groceries you buy twice because you forgot you had them. The fix, planning forward from what you already have, turned out to apply to the whole house, not just the pantry.

What's Inside

Forage is organised around a home, not a database:

  • Home Profile & Upkeep — your home's systems and the maintenance that keeps them running, so nothing sneaks up on you.
  • The Kitchen — stock across fridge, freezer, pantry, and spice rack (added by barcode, receipt, voice, camera, or typing), meal plans built from what you actually have, and shopping lists that carry only the gap.
  • The Board — the household's shared hub: notes, nudges, chore wars, and one-tap meal ratings. Those ratings are the preference engine — Forage learns your table over time instead of asking you to fill out a profile.
  • Ask Anything — a conversational assistant that answers questions about your own home and kitchen, not the whole internet.
  • Find the right help — local services and trades, coming soon.

How It's Built

Forage is a Fastify and PostgreSQL app with a React and TypeScript front end (TanStack Router and Query, Radix, Tailwind, Zustand). Every entity is household-scoped and syncs live across devices over WebSockets, with push notifications for the nudges that matter. Two models sit behind the assistant and the recipe author — Claude and Gemini.

The whole thing is self-hosted with data kept in Canada: no cloud vendor lock-in, no third-party trackers, PIPEDA-aligned. Private by design isn't a badge bolted on at the end — it's why the architecture looks the way it does.

The AI Angle

Because everything in Forage runs through a clean REST API, I built a companion Model Context Protocol (MCP) server on top of it. It lets AI agents like Claude manage your stock, plan meals, and review the shopping list through typed, validated tools — the same actions you'd take in the app, handed to an agent. It ships with prebuilt workflows like weekly meal prep, a pantry audit, and "use it up." That server is the Forage MCP entry over in the Lab.
