The Voyager: a small floating canoe

Workshop42 project notes

TypeFloating canoe model
Process3D printing
DesignContrasting colors
NotesProject notes

A small canoe made for play

The Voyager is a small 3D-printed canoe that floats. It's a quick project for someone who wants a little boat for the water, a desk decoration or a keychain, with a familiar canoe shape and a few bright colors.

The design keeps the project approachable: there's a solid-color file, a pre-painted multicolor option and a keychain version. The goal is a small, enjoyable print with something to do once it comes off the bed.

Making the shape read

The tapered ends and open interior give the little model a recognizable canoe shape. In the project photograph, red and yellow hulls get contrasting white rims and crosspieces, making the details easy to see at this scale. The hull is also made to float; the print notes below explain the wall setting recommended for better watertight performance.

Before you print

The listing describes three options: a pre-painted AMS version, a solid-color version and a keychain version with a loop. It suggests PLA or PETG and no supports. For better watertight performance, the notes recommend increasing the walls to three or four when the canoe will float.

The description gives a roughly 30-minute print with 0.2 mm layers and 15% infill. The currently displayed designer profile instead lists 0.16 mm layers, three walls and 10% infill, so check the actual file and slicer estimate for the version you choose. This is a small floating model, not a rated carrying vessel.
