Learning through the build

Nexus is my independent AI orchestration project and an applied-learning platform. It gives me a place to explore AI systems through hands-on software work.

The workshop, in software

The emphasis is on learning through implementation: taking an idea, building something that can be examined, and using what I learn to shape the next iteration.

It belongs in the same workshop as the printed prototypes, even though its materials are software and AI. It is independent project work in progress.
