The Voyager: a small floating canoe

TypeFloating canoe model
Process3D printing
DesignContrasting colors
NotesLocal review draft

A little room for play

The Voyager is a small floating canoe toy designed as a quick print. It brings a playful object into the workshop alongside the practical tools and desk projects.

A familiar form

The project image shows two color variations: red and yellow hulls, each with a contrasting white rim and crosspieces. The open interior and tapered ends keep the familiar canoe shape clear.

This project is about a recognizable form and a simple physical object that can be printed and enjoyed.
