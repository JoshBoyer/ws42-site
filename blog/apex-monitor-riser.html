Apex: printed parts meet a wooden shelf

Workshop42 project notesLocal review draft

TypeMonitor riser
PartsFour printed legs + wood board
DesignTriangular supports
NotesLocal review draft

Two materials, one useful object

Apex combines four printed legs with a wooden board to make a monitor riser. The printed parts form the supports; the board becomes the top.

The printed part

The triangular legs give the riser its geometric outline. Countersunk screw holes provide the attachment points beneath the board. Raising the shelf leaves space below for a keyboard and other desk items.

The project photo shows the same leg design in several colors. It is an example of using a printed component alongside an ordinary material rather than printing the whole object.

The original model

The Apex Monitor Riser is shared by Workshop42 on MakerWorld. The listing has the original model and print-profile information.
