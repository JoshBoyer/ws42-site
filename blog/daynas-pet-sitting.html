A website for a pet-care business

I built the Dayna's Pet Sitting website for a friend. It gives pet owners a place to understand the services and send a request with their dates and care needs, and gives the business a public home for that information.

The request form offers an estimate and starts a conversation; it doesn't present the request as a confirmed booking. That makes the next step clear for someone looking for care.

From a service to a request

Visitors can explore check-ins, dog walks and overnight care before using the form to describe what they need. The site puts the services and the request process in one place, so a visitor can move from looking at options to starting a conversation.

How the pieces fit together

Gatsby builds the React site, querying Sanity content with GraphQL. Styled-components and Material UI provide interface styling and components. The site framework builds the interface, while the content source supplies the information it displays.
