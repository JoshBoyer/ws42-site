The process.

Ideas become real. Then improve a little.

  1. 01

    Sketch

    Work out what needs to move, fit or connect.

  2. 02

    Print

    Make something physical to test the idea.

  3. 03

    Test

    Check the fit and the mechanism.

  4. 04

    Adjust

    Keep what works. Change what does not.
